Project · IT Service Management

Incident Operations & Digital Transformation

Analysis of 24,918 historical IT service incidents and 141,712 event records to investigate resolution delays, repeated handling and support-team routing patterns. The findings informed practical recommendations for improving incident management and operational efficiency.

SQL Python Power BI Technology Consulting Digital Transformation
24,918 Historical incidents analysed
22.1h Median incident resolution time
3,231 Observed routing returns

Management recommendation: Prioritise reviews of repeated incident handling, clarify support-team ownership and improve data-quality controls. Test proposed changes through a controlled 12-week pilot before considering wider implementation.

The analysis identifies historical associations, not proven causes of delays. No operational changes have been implemented, and potential efficiency improvements remain unverified.

Project · M&A Regulatory Intelligence

EU Merger Control Analysis & M&A Diligence Strategy

Analysis of 10,376 historical European Commission merger cases to examine regulatory outcomes, review durations and sector-level conditional-clearance patterns. The findings informed practical recommendations for preparing regulatory due diligence before an acquisition.

SQL Python Power BI M&A Consulting Regulatory Intelligence
10,376 Historical merger cases analysed
3.09% Phase II initiation among eligible cases
31 / 173 days Phase I / II median observed review intervals

Management recommendation: Assess regulatory filing requirements, investigate material competition overlaps and prepare review-timing contingencies. Use the proposed DCES prioritisation framework and 90-day diligence roadmap to guide decisions.

Historical statistical associations do not predict individual merger outcomes. Recommendations are proposed; no client implementation or regulatory approval is claimed.
